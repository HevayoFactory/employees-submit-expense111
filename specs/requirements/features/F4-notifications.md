# Notifications

## Purpose

Users are told when a claim needs their attention or its status changes.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories

- F4.1 As a manager, I receive an email when a team member submits a claim.
- F4.2 As an employee, I receive an email when my claim is approved.
- F4.3 As an employee, I receive an email when my claim is rejected, including the reason.

## Decisions

- Notifications are sent by email, using a transactional email service.
- Finance is not notified.

## Out of Scope

- In-app notifications.
