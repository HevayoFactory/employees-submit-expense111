# Approvals

## Purpose

Managers review and approve or reject their team's submitted claims.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories

- F2.1 As a manager, I see my team's pending claims in one list, oldest first. *assumed*
- F2.2 As a manager, I open a claim to see its lines and receipts.
- F2.3 As a manager, I approve a claim.
- F2.4 As a manager, I reject a claim with a reason.

## Decisions

- A claim is approved only by the submitting employee's line manager.
- A rejection requires a reason.
- A manager sees only claims of their own team. *assumed*

## Out of Scope

- Deputies approving while the manager is away.
- Finance or higher-level approval above an amount.
